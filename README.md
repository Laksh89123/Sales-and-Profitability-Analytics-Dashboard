# Sales and Profitability Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI portfolio project for exploring sales, profitability, regional performance, product and category results, monthly trends, and order-level metrics.

## Project File

- **Power BI report:** [`Project_Sales_Profitability.pbix`](Project_Sales_Profitability.pbix)
- **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Analysis period shown in the report:** 2023–2025

Download the PBIX file and open it in Power BI Desktop to explore the report interactively.

## Dashboard Pages

1. **Executive Overview** — KPI cards for total sales, cost of goods sold (COGS), total profit, profit margin, orders, quantity sold and average order value, plus monthly sales trend and sales by category.
2. **Geographical Analysis** — sales by country, region and zone; profit by region; a country sales map; and profit contribution by zone.
3. **Product Performance** — top products by sales and profit, sales-versus-profit comparison, and product profit margin.
4. **Category Analysis** — sales, profit, sales-versus-profit, category contribution and profit margin by category.
5. **Time Intelligence** — monthly sales trend, month-over-month (MoM) sales growth, year-over-year (YoY) sales growth and cumulative sales trend.
6. **Orders Analysis** — order-level details, order and quantity KPIs, average order value, monthly order trend, and top orders by sales and profit.
7. **Executive Summary & Recommendations** — headline KPIs, key business insights and follow-up recommendations.

## Initial Findings

- **Home Appliances** is the leading category by sales (0.57M) and category profit (193K) in the displayed report.
- **West** is the leading region by sales (0.55M), followed by **South** (0.45M).
- **Furniture** has the lowest displayed category profit margin (23.84%); this is a signal for further analysis, not proof of a specific cause.
- The time-intelligence page tracks monthly sales, MoM and YoY growth, and cumulative sales over the period shown.

## Business Questions Explored

- Which countries, regions, zones, categories and products contribute the most sales and profit?
- How do sales and profit margins differ across categories and products?
- How do monthly sales and growth rates change over time?
- Which orders rank highest by sales and profit?
- Which low-margin areas warrant additional investigation?

## Tools and Skills Demonstrated

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Interactive report design with KPI cards, slicers, bar charts, line charts, a donut chart, a map and a detail table
- DAX-based calculations and a calculated category/zone display column
- Time-series, regional, product, category and profitability analysis
- Translating chart findings into business questions and recommendations

## Recommendations Approach

Recommendations in the report are proposed follow-up actions, not confirmed root causes. For example, the lower Furniture margin should be investigated using relevant product-level sales, costs, pricing and discount data before deciding on corrective action.
